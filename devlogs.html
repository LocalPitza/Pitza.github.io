🎮 Welcome to DevLogs CMS!

How to use:

  1. Click [LOGIN] in the navigation (top-right)
  2. Enter the master password (set in script.js)
  3. Click "Create New DevLog" to add your first devlog
  4. Fill in the form with your development updates
  5. Upload screenshots if you have them
  6. Click "Save DevLog" to publish

Your devlogs are stored locally in your browser. Use the Export feature to backup!
