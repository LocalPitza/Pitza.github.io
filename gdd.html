Educational Objectives:

This document outlines how gameplay mechanics teach real-world gardening concepts through simplified gameplay: plant life cycles, soil management, sustainable practices, and urban farming techniques.

Design Philosophy:

  • Accessibility First: Simple controls with deep mechanics
  • Positive Reinforcement: Reward system for learning
  • Progressive Complexity: Gradual introduction of systems
  • Educational Learning: Creating a gateway to educational knowledge through the game
